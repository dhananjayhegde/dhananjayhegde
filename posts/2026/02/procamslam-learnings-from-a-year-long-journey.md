# The Idea of Procam Slam

I had a cartilege injury in my right ankle towards the end of 2024. I was out of running since November that year. Feb 2025 is when I was back with a 10k training plan again and started training for KSP 10k and in March and then TCS World 10K in Bengaluru in April. At this time, I was following the Garmin Coach running plan by Coach Greg. My plan was to run sub 50 10k.

This training proved to be fruitful in that I could get a PB in TCS world 10k by completing it in 50:37, only about a minute faster than my previous PB 51:20 in November 2024.

After completing it is when the idea of Slam was planted by Amrutha, who was attempting it for the 2nd year for her first attempt was sabotaged by an injury.

It was simple - start with TCS 10k in April --> Vedanta Delhi HM in Delhi in October --> Tata Steel World 25k in Kolkata in December --> Most coveted Tata Mumbai Marathon in January 2026.
